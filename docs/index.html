FROM BASE STATIONS TO SENSING DATA

Explore measured UAV echoes
in their real environment.

CellularEye records target-present echoes and target-free backgrounds using OFDM reference signals on outdoor base stations. UAV positioning, RGB/infrared video, and meteorological records document the collection conditions.

The toolkit reads binary measurements, forms range–Doppler maps, visualizes flights and collection windows, and exports position-assisted detection annotations.

THE MEASUREMENT PLATFORM

An outdoor, multi-site testbed.

A 228 m × 70 m site with four base-station locations, UAV facilities, visual sensors, and weather monitoring.

Cones illustrate beam geometry, not measured coverage boundaries. The current archived mmWave folders contain BS22 and BS23 measurements; the four-site layout describes the platform. Associated records vary by collection.

MEASURED SCENARIOS

See the returns. Compare the conditions.

Continuous UAV-visible intervals with full range and velocity axes. Select an animation to play it.

Red windows follow coarse RTK geometry and are 40 × 40 RD bins. The clips retain every frame in their selected interval: sunny frames 24–50 and rainy frames 22–51 of 52 recorded frames. These display windows differ from the benchmark’s 30 × 30-bin labels.
